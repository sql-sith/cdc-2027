# Welcome and Internet Overview

Our first meeting of the 2026–2027 season.

## What we covered

- **Who we are.** A club that studies Linux, Windows, cyber defense and related topics, and competes in two competitions in the spring. We are *not* a programming class, an AI workshop, or a cyber offense team, though we touch on all three.
- **Pop quizzes.** Who are you and why are you here? What makes a team member valuable? The answers came back to teamwork, curiosity, regular attendance, and putting in the effort. A member who doesn't know much yet has the most room to grow, which is the point of the "every student has value" chart.
- **How the Internet works.** A high-level walk through the diagram: your device asks for an IP address (DHCP), looks up the site's address (DNS), hands packets to your router (the default gateway), which passes them through a chain of routers to the destination network and its servers. The goal was to understand *what* happens, not yet *how*.
